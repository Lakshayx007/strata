# Phase 3 Summary: Market & Pipeline

**Workstream 1: Market Model**
- **$121.3B**: Forecasted total addressable market size by 2030 (up from $54.4B in 2025).
- **17.4%**: Overall CAGR for the enterprise data platform market.
- **50,000**: Estimated large-enterprise firms globally forming the core market.
- **60%**: Assumed adoption rate of modern data platforms in large enterprises.
- **SQL Analytics & BI**: Remains the largest workload segment through 2030.
- **AI/ML & GenAI Prep**: The fastest-growing workload (35% CAGR).
- **Public Cloud**: Dominates deployment, but hybrid remains significant (15% CAGR).
- **$33.5B Gap**: Bottom-up estimate exceeds top-down vendor revenue (Snowflake + Databricks + Cloud) because it captures internal spend and long-tail vendors.

**Workstream 2: Competitive Matrix**
- **24 Capabilities**: Evaluated across 6 vendors (Snowflake, Databricks, Cloudera, AWS, Microsoft, Google).
- **Cloudera Moat**: The only vendor scoring 2 (GA native) on all 5 deployment flexibility capabilities (on-prem, private cloud, sovereign, multi-cloud, single control plane).
- **Databricks Lead**: Highest combined score in GenAI agents, model serving, and open formats (Delta + UniForm).
- **Snowflake Gap**: Lacks on-prem deployment and cross-system lineage, limiting reach in heavily regulated air-gapped environments.
- **Governance Leaders**: Databricks (Unity OSS), Microsoft (Purview), and Cloudera (Atlas) lead in cross-system lineage.
- **Openness Axis**: Cloud vendors cluster toward flexible cloud but inflexible on-prem, while Cloudera occupies the "Open and flexible" quadrant.

**Workstream 3: CRM Simulation**
- **4,000**: Simulated pipeline opportunities across 1,500 accounts over 8 quarters.
- **32%**: Cost is the leading loss reason, calibrated exactly to phase-1 stated-reason shares.
- **17.5%**: Operational complexity is the second-most frequent reason for a lost deal.
- **8% Churn**: Simulated baseline churn rate.
- **30% Expansion**: Simulated expansion rate for retained accounts.
- **35% Win Rate**: Average win rate across the simulated pipeline.
- **72% On-time**: Average delivery velocity across simulated PM teams, with a ~4-day average slip.
